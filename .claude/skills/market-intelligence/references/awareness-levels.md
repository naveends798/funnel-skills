# Eugene Schwartz's 5 Levels of Awareness

From *Breakthrough Advertising* (1966). Every prospect sits at exactly one level when they encounter your funnel. The hook that converts a Level 1 buyer is invisible to a Level 5 buyer — and vice versa.

## Level 1 — Most Aware

**Knows:** Your brand, your product, the price.
**State:** Ready to buy. Just needs the right offer or moment.
**Language:** Specific product names, model numbers, comparison shopping language ("vs", "discount", "deal").
**Hook pattern:** Lead with **the offer**. Price + bonus + scarcity. No selling required.

> "Get [Product] today — 30% off until midnight + free shipping."

---

## Level 2 — Product Aware

**Knows:** Your product exists. Doesn't know if it's right for them.
**State:** Comparing. Reading reviews. On the fence.
**Language:** "Is X worth it?", "X vs Y", "Does X work for [specific situation]?"
**Hook pattern:** Lead with **proof + differentiation**. Show why your version wins for their specific case.

> "The [Product] that finally worked for [specific audience] — here's the difference."

---

## Level 3 — Solution Aware

**Knows:** A category of solution exists. Doesn't know about your specific product.
**State:** Researching options. Open but not committed.
**Language:** "Best [solution category]", "How to [solve problem]", "[Solution] for [audience]".
**Hook pattern:** Lead with **the unique mechanism**. Why your approach beats the category.

> "Why most [category] fail — and the [unique mechanism] that fixes it."

---

## Level 4 — Problem Aware

**Knows:** They have a problem. Doesn't know solutions exist.
**State:** Frustrated. Possibly hopeless. Looking for hope.
**Language:** "Why does X keep happening?", "I've tried everything", "Is there any way to...?"
**Hook pattern:** Lead with **the problem named precisely**, then promise a solution exists.

> "If you've tried [thing 1, thing 2, thing 3] and you're still [problem state], the issue isn't you — it's [unique angle]."

---

## Level 5 — Unaware

**Knows:** Nothing. Doesn't realize they have a problem.
**State:** Going about life. No active search. Hardest to convert.
**Language:** Not searching at all. Reached via interruption (ads, content, virality).
**Hook pattern:** Lead with **a story or a curiosity gap**. Earn the first 5 seconds before introducing the problem.

> "Last Tuesday a [unexpected character] did [unexpected thing] and the [outcome] surprised everyone."

---

## Application to funnels

- **Cold ad traffic** is usually Level 4–5. Lead with story, problem-naming, or pattern interrupt.
- **Organic content traffic** (YouTube, blog) is usually Level 3–4. Lead with mechanism or named problem.
- **Email list traffic** is usually Level 2–3. Lead with proof and differentiation.
- **Existing customer traffic** is Level 1. Lead with offer.

The same product needs **different headlines** at different traffic temperatures. The market-intelligence output's `awareness_levels` array gives the copywriter the language for each level so they can write hooks that land on each one.
