# VSL 12-beat skeleton

This skeleton ships VSLs at 12–18 minutes. It hits every conversion checkpoint a buyer needs.

| Beat | Name | Duration | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook | 0:00–0:30 | Earn the next 30 seconds. Pattern interrupt + promise. |
| 2 | Problem | 0:30–2:00 | Name the pain so they say "yes, that's me". |
| 3 | Stakes | 2:00–3:30 | What it's costing them. Twist the knife (carefully). |
| 4 | Origin | 3:30–5:00 | Founder's story — "I was where you are now". Builds trust. |
| 5 | Discovery | 5:00–7:00 | The "aha moment" that led to the unique mechanism. |
| 6 | Mechanism | 7:00–9:00 | How the system actually works. The big idea revealed. |
| 7 | Proof | 9:00–11:00 | Case studies + numbers + testimonials. |
| 8 | Offer | 11:00–13:00 | What they get inside. Stack reveal. |
| 9 | Bonus stack | 13:00–14:00 | Fast-action bonuses. |
| 10 | Guarantee | 14:00–14:30 | Risk reversal. |
| 11 | CTA | 14:30–15:30 | Specific next action. Repeat the URL/button. |
| 12 | Close + Reminder | 15:30–16:00 | One-line summary. Final CTA. |

---

## Beat-by-beat detail

### Beat 1 — Hook (30 sec)

The first 30 seconds decide whether they watch or click away. Patterns that work:

- **Curiosity gap:** "In the next 14 minutes I'll show you the exact [thing] [N] [audience] used to [outcome]. The system is so [counterintuitive] you'll wonder why nobody told you sooner."
- **Story:** "Last [time period] I was [bad state]. [Specific moment]. Today [outcome]."
- **Promise + qualifier:** "If you're [audience] and you've tried [thing 1, thing 2, thing 3] and you're still [stuck state], this video is the difference."

End with a forward promise: "By the end of this video you'll know exactly [what]."

### Beat 2 — Problem (1.5 min)

Pull from `01-market.json` pain_points. Name 3–5 specific symptoms in the audience's language. NO generic "are you tired of" framing.

### Beat 3 — Stakes (1.5 min)

Cost of doing nothing. Financial, emotional, time, relational. 3 concrete consequences:
- "If you keep [doing wrong thing] for 12 more months, you'll [bad outcome]."
- "Every week without [solution] means [specific cost]."
- "By the time you figure this out the hard way, [external pressure] will have already happened."

Don't pile on. The buyer already feels bad — your job is to crystallize, not torment.

### Beat 4 — Origin (1.5 min)

The founder's story. Concrete moments. Use intake's "story" or "background" if present.

Pattern:
> "I used to be [audience profile]. I tried [thing 1] for [time]. Failed. Then [thing 2]. Failed. Then a [specific moment / mentor / discovery] changed everything."

Builds permission to teach. Don't skip — VSLs without origin underperform.

### Beat 5 — Discovery (2 min)

The aha moment that led to the unique mechanism. This is where you differentiate from competitors.

> "I noticed that everyone teaching [topic] focused on [conventional approach]. But the [N] who actually got [outcome] all did one thing differently — they [the unique angle]."

### Beat 6 — Mechanism (2 min)

How the system works. Use the System Lens (Input → Process → Output) or a 3-step framework.

> "Here's how the [mechanism] works:
> Step 1 — [what goes in].
> Step 2 — [what happens inside].
> Step 3 — [what comes out].
> The reason it works is [the underlying principle]."

### Beat 7 — Proof (2 min)

Stack 3–5 proof elements:
- A named case study with numbers
- A second case study from a different segment
- An aggregate metric ("We've used this with [N] [audience] and the average [outcome] is [number]")
- Optional: an authority signal (publication, mentor, certification)

### Beat 8 — Offer (2 min)

The stack. Walk through every line item with stated value.

Pattern:
> "When you join [Offer Name], you get:
> Module 1 — [name]. This walks you through [what]. ($X value.)
> Module 2 — [name]. ...
> ..."

End with the total stated value.

### Beat 9 — Bonuses (1 min)

2–3 fast-action bonuses. "Decide today and you also get…"

### Beat 10 — Guarantee (30 sec)

State the guarantee in one sentence. Add the reassurance line. Don't undersell — read the guarantee like you mean it.

### Beat 11 — CTA (1 min)

Specific URL. Repeat it. Tell them exactly what happens when they click ("you'll go to a checkout page, you'll see the order form, you'll be inside in 2 minutes").

> "Click the button below this video. Or go to [URL]. You'll see the order form. Use the credit card you trust most. The next 60 seconds is the longest delay between you and [outcome]."

### Beat 12 — Close (30 sec)

One paragraph. Restate the promise from Beat 1. Restate the URL.

> "If you've watched this far, you already know [solution] is the answer. The only question is whether you'll [act] today or [delay]. I built this for the [audience] who decide. Click below."
