# Funnel pattern catalog

Each pattern has a sweet-spot price range, awareness fit, and known conversion benchmarks. Pick the pattern that matches the offer — don't force a webinar onto a $47 product.

---

## 1. Tripwire / SLO (Self-Liquidating Offer)

**Price range:** $7 – $97
**Best for:** Building a buyer list cheaply. The tripwire breaks even on ad spend; profit comes from upsells and the back-end.
**Stages:** Ad → Landing page (or sales page) → Order form → Order bump → Upsell 1 → Upsell 2 → Thank you
**Benchmark conversion:** 2–6% landing → tripwire. 30–60% take order bump. 10–25% take upsell 1.
**Awareness fit:** Levels 2–4 work. Level 5 (unaware) is hard at low price points unless the hook is unmissable.

---

## 2. VSL Sales Page

**Price range:** $200 – $3,000
**Best for:** Info products, courses, software with a clear before/after.
**Stages:** Ad/email → VSL landing page → Order form → (optional upsell)
**Benchmark conversion:** 1–4% on cold, 5–10% on warm. VSL completion rate is the primary lever (people who watch 70%+ buy at 4–8× the rate of skimmers).
**Length sweet spot:** 12–25 minutes. Sub-10-minute VSLs underperform; 40+ minute VSLs lose audiences.

---

## 3. Webinar (live or evergreen)

**Price range:** $497 – $5,000
**Best for:** Higher-ticket info products, coaching, masterminds. Takes longer to convert but commands higher prices.
**Stages:** Ad → Webinar registration → Email reminders → Webinar (60–90 min) → Pitch (last 15 min) → Order page → 1:1 sales call (optional)
**Benchmark conversion:** 30–55% registration → show-up. 2–8% show-up → buyer (live higher than evergreen).
**Lead time:** registration to webinar = 2–7 days. Long-tail email follow-up adds 30–50% more buyers over 14 days.

---

## 4. 5-Day Challenge

**Price range:** $97 – $2,000 (challenge usually free, sells the upsell at end)
**Best for:** Coaching, fitness, high-energy education niches. Builds community and indoctrination.
**Stages:** Ad → Challenge opt-in → Email + SMS daily for 5 days → Day 5 pitch → Order page → 1:1 follow-up
**Benchmark conversion:** 40–70% opt-in completion (the 30% who finish are highly qualified). 5–15% finishers → buyer at premium price.
**Effort:** highest — requires daily live or pre-recorded sessions for 5 days.

---

## 5. Quiz Funnel

**Price range:** Any. Especially good for ambiguous-need niches (skincare, supplements, business coaching).
**Best for:** Buyers who don't know which solution they need. The quiz segments and recommends.
**Stages:** Ad → Quiz (3–8 questions) → Email capture for results → Personalized result page → Offer matched to result
**Benchmark conversion:** 15–35% start-to-finish. 5–12% quiz takers → buyer.
**Bonus:** generates rich first-party segmentation data for retargeting.

---

## 6. Book / Lead Magnet Funnel

**Price range:** Free (book = lead magnet) → $97–$497 (next product) → $2,000+ (back-end)
**Best for:** Authority builders. The book/PDF is a credibility weapon, not a profit center.
**Stages:** Ad → Free book opt-in → Order form (often free + S&H) → Order bump → Upsell → Long-term email nurture
**Benchmark conversion:** 15–40% opt-in (low because it's free). The play is the back-end, not the front.

---

## 7. Application Funnel (high-ticket)

**Price range:** $5,000+
**Best for:** Coaching programs, mastermind groups, agency services.
**Stages:** Ad → Landing page (no price) → Application form → Booking calendar → Sales call → Close
**Benchmark conversion:** 0.5–2% landing → application. 30–60% application → call show. 15–35% calls → close.
**Why no price on landing:** filters tire-kickers. Price reveal happens on the call.

---

## 8. Two-Step Order Form (impulse)

**Price range:** $7 – $47
**Best for:** Trip wires where simplicity beats persuasion.
**Stages:** Ad → 2-step order form (email first, then payment) → Thank you
**Benchmark conversion:** 1–3% cold ad → buyer. The 2-step format lifts conversion 20–35% over single-step.

---

## Pattern selection cheat sheet

| Price | Audience awareness | Pattern |
|---|---|---|
| < $50 | Any | Tripwire / 2-step |
| $50–$500 | Levels 2–4 | Tripwire → Upsell or VSL |
| $500–$3K | Levels 2–4 | VSL or Webinar |
| $500–$3K | Levels 4–5 | 5-Day Challenge or Webinar |
| $3K–$10K | Any | Webinar → 1:1 booking |
| $10K+ | Any | Application funnel |
| Ambiguous niche | Any | Quiz funnel |
| Authority play | Any | Book funnel |
