# Sequence structures

Every sequence has a job. Don't blur jobs across sequences.

## Welcome sequence (5 emails)

**Job:** indoctrinate. Build trust. Tee up the offer without hard-selling.
**Length:** 5 days, one email per day.
**Open rate target:** 50%+ (engaged audience just opted in)
**Conversion target:** 2–6% from list to first purchase

### Email 1 — Delivery & Expectation Setting
- Subject: deliver the magnet ("Your [Thing] is inside")
- Body: thank, deliver link, set expectation ("Over the next 4 days I'll send…"), one-line about who you are.
- CTA: download / read the magnet

### Email 2 — Origin Story
- Subject: a personal hook ("I almost quit before [moment]")
- Body: founder origin tied to audience pain. End with a lesson learned that previews the mechanism.
- CTA: soft — reply with the biggest version of the problem

### Email 3 — The Big Idea
- Subject: the contrarian frame ("Why most [category] advice is wrong")
- Body: teach one slice of the unique mechanism. Be specific — give them an actionable insight.
- CTA: link to a related blog/video, or soft pitch

### Email 4 — Case Study
- Subject: "[Name] went from [bad state] to [good state] in [time]"
- Body: structured case — situation, intervention, result. End with what made it work.
- CTA: link to the offer ("Want the same playbook? Start here")

### Email 5 — The Pitch
- Subject: "If you want the full system…"
- Body: name the offer, list 3–5 stack highlights, restate the guarantee, call out who it's for and who it's NOT for.
- CTA: hard — visit the sales page

## Nurture sequence (7 emails)

**Job:** stay top-of-mind for 2–4 weeks for non-buyers. Build goodwill that leads to eventual conversion.
**Cadence:** 1 email per week (sometimes 2 in early stages).
**Conversion target:** 1–3% per email of the unconverted segment.

### Topics rotation (pick 7 from this list)
1. **Tactical tip** — one actionable thing they can do today
2. **Contrarian take** — a hot take that polarizes (drives replies)
3. **Behind-the-scenes** — a build, a metric, a process from your work
4. **Reader Q&A** — a real question + your answer
5. **Tool / resource** — something useful they can use independently
6. **Results from students** — short case study (2–3 paragraphs)
7. **Personal lesson** — a failure/win and what it taught
8. **Industry update** — what's changing, what to do about it
9. **Reading / listening** — book, podcast, channel recommendation

Each email ends with a 1-line soft CTA: "If you want the full system, [the offer link]."

## Sales sequence (5 emails)

**Job:** convert during a launch window. Tight 5-day arc.
**Cadence:** one email per day.
**Conversion target:** 5–15% of engaged list converts during a 5-day launch.

### Day 1 — Launch Open
- Subject: announce ("Doors are open: [Offer Name]")
- Body: why-now (cohort start, price-up, end of beta). Outcome promise. Stack highlights.
- CTA: visit the sales page

### Day 2 — Objection #1: Price
- Subject: "The real cost of [problem they're not solving]"
- Body: math the cost of NOT solving the problem. Frame the offer price as the cheaper option.
- CTA: payment plan reminder

### Day 3 — Big Proof
- Subject: "[N] people, [outcome]"
- Body: case study + numbers + testimonial montage.
- CTA: order

### Day 4 — FAQ + Bonuses
- Subject: "Top 5 questions about [Offer Name]"
- Body: address the top 5 objections from the FAQ. Reveal the fast-action bonus stack.
- CTA: order with bonus

### Day 5 — Last Call
- Subject: "Last call: [Offer] closes at [time]"
- Body: specific deadline. What happens if they wait (price up, cohort closed). One paragraph max.
- CTA: order now

## Post-purchase sequence (4 emails)

**Job:** reduce refunds, drive engagement, set up the next purchase.
**Cadence:** instant → 1 day → 7 days → 30 days.
**Refund-rate target:** under 5% (industry average is 6–10%).

### Hour 0 — Receipt + Onboarding
- Subject: "Welcome inside [Offer]. Start here."
- Body: receipt + login link + "the first thing to do (takes 15 minutes)"
- CTA: log in / open the first module

### Day 1 — Quick Win
- Subject: "Your first win (do this today)"
- Body: a tactic from inside the program that delivers value within 30 minutes.
- CTA: take the action, then reply with the result

### Day 7 — Check-In + Community
- Subject: "[Name], how's it going?"
- Body: ask for a status, point them to the community / next module / a piece of help.
- CTA: visit the community

### Day 30 — Results Check + Next Tier
- Subject: "Your 30-day check-in"
- Body: ask what they've achieved. Point to a next-tier offer (mastermind, 1:1, advanced cohort).
- CTA: book a call / upgrade
