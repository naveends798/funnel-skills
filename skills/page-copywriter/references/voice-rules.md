# Voice rules

The page must sound like the client wrote it — not like ChatGPT pasted into a builder.

## Read the intake's `voice` and `tone` field

If the field exists, follow it literally. Examples of how to translate vibes:

| If voice says... | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| "Warm, grounded" | Short sentences. Personal pronouns. Specifics. | Hype punctuation, exclamation pile-ups |
| "Direct, contrarian" | Hot takes, named-out competitors, statistics | Hedging ("might", "could", "perhaps") |
| "Premium, professional" | Confident statements, no caps-lock, refined vocab | Slang, emojis, "literally" |
| "Playful, casual" | Rhythm, asides, lowercase headers OK | Stiff corporate phrasing |
| "Technical, builder" | Specifics, system framing, "under the hood" | Hand-wavy claims, motivational filler |

## Universal don'ts (regardless of voice)

- "Are you tired of [X]?" — lazy hook
- "Imagine if..." — limp opener
- "In today's fast-paced world" — cliché
- "Take your [thing] to the next level" — meaningless
- "Game-changing" / "revolutionary" / "cutting-edge" — adjective inflation
- Emoji bullets in body copy (occasionally OK in CTA buttons)
- Long flowing sentences if the voice is short and direct

## Universal do's

- One idea per sentence in the hero. Reader is skimming.
- Concrete > abstract. "$2,400" > "a lot of money". "Friday at 3pm" > "soon".
- Active voice > passive voice. "We built it" not "It was built".
- Re-read every sentence by speaking it aloud (mentally). If it doesn't sound like a person talking, rewrite.

## Sentence length cadence

Mix short and medium. A landing page that's all 6-word sentences feels jagged. All 25-word sentences put readers to sleep.

Pattern that works:
> Short. Medium-length sentence with a specific detail. Even shorter. Then a longer one that ties it together with rhythm and gives the reader a place to breathe before the next punchy line. Cut.

## Voice-test before shipping

Read 3 random sentences aloud as if YOU were the client. If you cringe, rewrite. The cringe test catches 80% of off-voice copy.
