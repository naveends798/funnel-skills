# The Offer Equation

From Alex Hormozi, *$100M Offers*. Every offer's perceived value is:

```
                Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood
Value =  ────────────────────────────────────────────────
              Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice
```

To raise value, attack each lever:

## 1. Dream Outcome — make it bigger and more specific

- "Lose weight" → "Drop 28 pounds before your daughter's wedding in March"
- "Get more clients" → "Add 6 inbound calls per week from your YouTube channel"
- "Save time" → "Reclaim Saturday mornings — funnel builds run while you sleep"

The more specific and emotionally connected the dream outcome, the higher the value.

## 2. Perceived Likelihood — stack proof

- Case studies with names + numbers + screenshots
- Methodology / unique mechanism (an explainable system, not "trust me")
- Guarantees — outcome > time > money-back > none
- Pre-commitment social proof ("847 people enrolled this month")
- Authority markers — published-in, worked-with, certified-by

## 3. Time Delay — compress it

- "Within 30 days" beats "eventually"
- "Day 1: setup. Day 2: first lead. Day 7: first sale." beats "in a few weeks"
- Quick wins inside the offer reduce perceived delay even if final outcome takes longer

## 4. Effort & Sacrifice — minimize it

- "Done-with-you" sells higher than "done-by-you"
- Templates, swipe files, scripts pre-made > "you'll figure it out"
- Concierge onboarding > self-serve
- "Drag and drop into your funnel builder" > "code it from scratch"

## Pattern: when you're stuck on price

If the price feels too low, you haven't pushed Dream Outcome hard enough.
If conversion is low, you haven't backed Perceived Likelihood with enough proof.
If people stall, Time Delay or Effort feels too high — compress it or hand-hold harder.

## Output guidance for offer-architect

Translate the equation into the value stack:
- Each line item in the stack should hit at least one lever explicitly
- The guarantee directly attacks Perceived Likelihood
- The "done-with-you" or "done-for-you" tier directly attacks Effort
- Specific timelines in the deliverables attack Time Delay
- The "after this you'll have…" promise attacks Dream Outcome
