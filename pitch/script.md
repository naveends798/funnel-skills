# funnel-skills — YouTube launch video script

**Length target:** 9–10 minutes
**Hook target:** 12 seconds (must land before 0:15)
**Vibe:** measured, confident, agency-owner-talking-to-agency-owners — not hypey
**Production note:** every section has minimal on-screen text. The pitch canvas at `pitch/index.html` is your B-roll — frame the animated sections behind your voiceover for the conceptual parts, and switch to live screen capture for the demo.

## 0:00–0:12 — THE HOOK (must land in 12 seconds)

**What's on screen:** Cold open. Camera-on-screen recording — your browser, 12 tabs visible. ChatGPT. Reddit. Notion. Google Docs. Trustpilot. Apify. ClickFunnels. Loom. You alt-tab fast for 2 seconds. Cut to black with the line.

**Voiceover (3 punchy beats, no breath between):**

> Building a funnel for a client takes a week.
> Twelve tabs. Eight tools. Three days of copy.
> I rebuilt the whole thing. Now it takes five minutes.

**On-screen text (white on black, big, fades in beat-by-beat):**
- 7 days
- 12 tabs
- 5 minutes ← stays up

**Why this works:** specificity ("12 tabs", "8 tools", "5 minutes") beats vague claims. The word "rebuilt" implies you didn't just slap something together — you went through the pain.

---

## 0:12–0:45 — THE FRUSTRATION (the before state)

**What's on screen:** pitch canvas §2 (`#problem`). The 10 stacked tabs animate in one by one as you speak. Your voiceover reads them out roughly in order.

**Voiceover:**

> Here's what building one funnel actually looks like.
>
> You open Reddit and Trustpilot to find pain points.
> You go to ChatGPT to write the offer.
> You whiteboard the funnel structure.
> Open another AI to generate hook ideas.
> Another one for sales copy.
> Try to write 21 emails — give up halfway, restart Tuesday.
> Block out two days for the VSL script.
> Brief a designer. Wait. Revise. Wait. Revise.
> Rebuild it in GoHighLevel or ClickFunnels or Framer.
>
> Six to eight days. Per. Client.

**Cut to you on camera, briefly:**

> If you've built more than three funnels, you know exactly what I'm describing. The work isn't the hard part. The *coordination* is the hard part. Half your time is copy-pasting between tools.

---

## 0:45–1:30 — BEFORE / AFTER

**What's on screen:** pitch canvas §3 (`#split`). The "Before" card and "After" card animate in side by side. Stat numbers count up (7 days, 5 minutes).

**Voiceover:**

> So I asked: what would this look like if it ran as one thing?
>
> No tabs. No copy-paste. No "wait, which tool was I using for the emails?"
>
> One slash command. You paste anything you have about the client — a URL, a discovery call PDF, scratch notes, all three mixed together. And five minutes later, every asset is on your machine.
>
> Page copy, ready to paste. 21 emails, written. VSL script, teleprompter-ready. Branded HTML page, rendered. Builder prompts for whichever platform you ship on.

**Pause.** Cut to you on camera.

> That's the version I built for myself. Then I packaged it so other people could install it.

---

## 1:30–2:10 — WHY I BUILT THIS

**What's on screen:** you on camera, plain talk. No heavy graphics. Maybe a single B-roll cutaway of a calendar with "JANE FITNESS — DUE THURSDAY" written on it.

**Voiceover:**

> Quick context: I run a small funnel agency. Every new client is the same dance — research, offer, copy, emails, VSL, design, build. By the time you hand it off, you've burned a full week.
>
> The thing nobody tells you about agency work is that the bottleneck isn't talent. It's *handoffs.* Every step waits on the previous step's output, in someone else's tool.
>
> So I sat down with Claude Code — the developer terminal AI from Anthropic — and built ten specialist agents. Each one does one thing really well. Together, they hand off to each other in sequence. The whole pipeline runs in five minutes.
>
> Then I wrapped them as a Claude Code plugin so anyone can install it in one command.

---

## 2:10–3:30 — WHAT IT DOES — THE TEN AGENTS

**What's on screen:** pitch canvas §5 (`#agents`). The 10 agent cards are visible in a grid. Highlight each one as you describe it (~7 seconds each).

**Voiceover (keep it brisk — one beat per agent):**

> Here's the lineup.
>
> **One — market intelligence.** Researches the niche. Builds the ICP. Maps the five awareness levels from Schwartz. Pulls real language from Reddit and Trustpilot.
>
> **Two — offer architect.** Builds the value stack, picks the pricing ladder, writes the guarantee, runs a competitor teardown.
>
> **Three — strategy advisor.** Picks the right funnel pattern. Webinar, VSL, tripwire, quiz, challenge — based on the offer and the audience.
>
> **Four — hook engineer.** Fifteen hooks plus three headline ladders, all mapped to awareness levels.
>
> **Five — page copywriter.** Three thousand five hundred words. Nine sections. Hero through final CTA. Ready to paste.
>
> **Six — email sequence architect.** Twenty-one emails across four sequences. Welcome, nurture, sales, post-purchase. Subject, preview, body, delay, CTA — for every one.
>
> **Seven — VSL scriptwriter.** Twelve beats. Hook to close. Teleprompter-ready cadence.
>
> **Eight — landing design.** Generates a branded HTML page *plus* paste-ready prompts for GoHighLevel AI Studio, ClickFunnels AI, and Framer AI.
>
> **Nine — funnel orchestrator.** This is the conductor. Reads your intake, runs the other agents in sequence, assembles the dashboard.
>
> **Ten — funnel doctor.** This one's different — it's *post-launch.* Once your funnel is live, you paste the URL and your real metrics, and it gives you a diagnostic punch list with realistic expected lifts.

---

## 3:30–4:00 — THE PIPELINE

**What's on screen:** pitch canvas §6 (`#pipeline`). The sequential→parallel flow.

**Voiceover:**

> Here's the trick that makes it fast: stages one through three are sequential — market research feeds the offer, offer feeds the strategy. But stage four runs four agents in parallel. Hooks, page copy, emails, and the VSL script all run simultaneously. Then stage five takes everything and renders the branded HTML page.
>
> Five stages. Roughly five minutes. The dashboard opens in your browser at the end.

## 4:00–7:00 — LIVE DEMO (the meat of the video)

**What's on screen:** screen capture of your actual machine. Open Claude Code. This is the section where viewers see this *isn't a mockup.*

### 4:00–4:30 — Install (30 seconds)

**Voiceover:**

> Okay, install. This is the entire process.

Type in Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add naveends798/funnel-skills
```

Wait for the green check. Then:

```
/plugin install funnel-skills@funnel-skills
```

> Restart Claude Code once. That's it. No terminal. No npm install. No symlinks. No "find your folder, then drag this here."

### 4:30–7:00 — Running /funnel-intake on a real example

**Setup:** have a `~/clients/strong-method-coaching` folder pre-made (empty). Open Claude Code in that folder. You'll use the included fitness-coach example for repeatability.

**Voiceover:**

> Each new client gets its own folder so the assets stay isolated. I'll use the fitness-coach example that ships with the plugin so you can run the same one yourself.

Type:
```
/funnel-intake
```

> Claude asks me what I have. I can paste a URL, a PDF path, plain notes, or a mix.

Paste an example block:
```
Strong Method Coaching
https://strongmethod.example.com
12-week 1:1 strength training for women 42-58
$1,997 or 3 × $700
Voice: warm, grounded, no hype
Brand: forest green, ochre, ivory
Fonts: Fraunces + Inter
```

> Claude parses it. Fetches the URL in the background. Pulls out brand details. Asks me to confirm.

When the confirmation prompt comes up:

> Everything looks right. I reply "go".

Now the streaming output begins. Don't fast-forward this part — viewers want to see real progress. **Do** narrate over it though, so it doesn't feel slow:

> ✓ market intelligence — that's the ICP and awareness levels.
> ✓ offer architected — pricing ladder is built.
> ✓ strategy: webinar funnel — for this offer at this price point, that's the right pattern.

> ✓ page copy — three thousand seven hundred words.
> ✓ 21 emails.
> ✓ VSL script — 12 beats, around 14 minutes.
> ✓ landing.html rendered.
> ✓ GoHighLevel, ClickFunnels, and Framer prompts.

When the dashboard auto-opens:

> And there it is. Every asset, in one folder, with copy buttons on every block.

## 8:30–9:00 — POST-LAUNCH AUDIT

**What's on screen:** screen capture of `/audit` running on a different (already-built) example client where you can paste fake but realistic metrics.

**Voiceover:**

> Once your funnel is live and you have real traffic data, you run `/audit`. Paste your live URL, your ad creative, and whatever metrics you have — CTR, CPC, conversion, ROAS, scroll depth, refund rate. Whatever you have.

Type:
```
/audit
```

Pick your client from the picker. Paste:

```
https://strongmethod.example.com/program
Ad: "Stop the cardio. Build strength while your hormones rebalance."
CTR 1.4%, CPC $4.20, ROAS 1.6x
12k sessions, 18% opt-in, 2.1% conversion, 47% bounce
Webinar show-up is 22%.
```

When the audit finishes:

> The audit cross-references your live page against the assets we generated. Walks the diagnostic tree — ad-page alignment, mid-page narrative drop-off, offer believability. Returns a punch list with specific fixes and realistic expected lifts.

> So this isn't a "build and forget" tool. It's a complete loop — build, ship, measure, fix, ship again.
